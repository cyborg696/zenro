Privacy

What we collect when you order, and what we do with it.

What we collect at checkout

  • Your name, mobile number and, if you give it, your email address
  • Your district, upazila or area, full address and any order note
  • The watches you order and your delivery zone
  • Your payment method and, for mobile or bank payments, the sender number, transaction ID (TrxID), depositor name or reference number

We use these details only to verify your payment, confirm your order and deliver it.

How your order reaches us

This website has no customer accounts and no database. When you tap “Send order on WhatsApp”, your order details are placed in a WhatsApp message to our number, 01960520872. You can read the message before you send it. WhatsApp’s own privacy policy applies to that message.

A copy of your order is also sent securely to our order form service, so that we keep a record of it.

What is stored in your browser

Your cart is saved in your browser’s local storage so it is still there when you come back. Your last order is kept in your browser’s session storage until you close the tab. You can clear both at any time in your browser settings.

This website does not use cookies, advertising trackers or analytics. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Your PIN and OTP

We never ask for your bKash, Nagad, Rocket or bank PIN, password or OTP. Never share them with anyone, including us.

Questions

To ask about your data or have it deleted, message us on WhatsApp.
